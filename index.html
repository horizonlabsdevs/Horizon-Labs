Clear by design.

Horizon Labs is a research lab building onchain transparency for tokenized assets. Markets close. Onchain doesn't.

Building in public. Early stage.

You can hold an asset without seeing what's behind it.

  • Backing is hard to verify

    A token says it represents an asset. Where is the proof, and how recently was it updated?

  • Information is scattered

    Reserves, supply and activity live across explorers, documents and social posts, rarely in one readable place.

  • Trust runs on claims

    Without an easy way to check, people rely on reputation and screenshots.

  • After hours is a gray zone

    When traditional markets are closed, the link between onchain price and underlying value is unclear.

What we're building

Tools that make tokenized assets legible, from one view of the facts to open data other builders can use.

Transparency dashboard

One place to inspect a tokenized asset: what backs it, how supply and reserves line up, and how that changes over time. Every figure shows its source and when it was last updated.

After-hours visibility

A clear view of onchain price activity while traditional markets are closed, so the gap between close and open is something you can observe.

Open tooling

Data and analytics other builders can use to make their own assets and products easier to inspect.

How we work

  • Clarity over complexity

    If a number can't be explained simply, it isn't ready to be shown.

  • Evidence over claims

    Show the source, the timestamp and the method.

  • Honest about limits

    Every dataset has gaps. We label them instead of hiding them.

  • Built in the open

    Progress, changes and lessons are shared as they happen.

  • Long-term over loud

    We would rather be useful next year than trending this week.

$HORIZON

$HORIZON is the community token of the Horizon Labs ecosystem. It connects people who care about transparent tokenized assets with the team building for them.

Utility and mechanics will be published only once they are final and real. We won't announce features before they exist, or attach promises to the token that the product can't support.

Roadmap

  1. Foundation

    Brand, website and community channels. Public documentation of the vision and principles. First prototype of the transparency view.

  2. Visibility

    Reserve and backing dashboard for selected tokenized assets. After-hours price visibility. Regular public progress updates.

  3. Openness

    Shared data and analytics tooling for other builders. Community input on which assets and metrics to cover next.

This is a direction, not a guarantee. Timelines and scope may change as we learn.
